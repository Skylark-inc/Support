Skylark on Unsupported Platforms

Skylark is only available on supported versions of iOS and iPadOS.

Skylark is not available on Android, Windows, Linux, ChromeOS, or any other unsupported platform.

Why?

Skylark is built as a Siri Shortcut and relies heavily on Apple's Shortcuts app and system integrations.

While basic shortcut functionality has existed since iOS 12, Skylark depends on newer actions introduced throughout later versions of iOS and iPadOS, beginning with iOS 14.3 and continuing with every major software release.

These actions are exclusive to Apple's ecosystem and are not available on Android, Windows, Linux, or other operating systems.

Because Skylark depends on Apple-specific technologies, it cannot run on unsupported platforms.

Will Skylark ever come to Android?

No.

Skylark is not planned for Android.

Any source claiming Skylark is available on Android is incorrect.

What about Windows or Linux?

No.

Skylark is not coming to Windows, Linux, ChromeOS, or other desktop operating systems.

These platforms do not provide the Apple frameworks and Shortcuts actions that Skylark requires.

Could Apple make it possible?

The only realistic way Skylark could appear on unsupported platforms would be if Apple expanded its Shortcuts platform beyond iOS, iPadOS, and supported Apple devices.

At this time, such a change is highly unlikely.

Are there alternatives?

Probably not.

This is not intended to encourage the purchase of an iPhone or iPad. Android, Windows, Linux, and other platforms offer their own tools, services, and built-in features.

Skylark does not provide workarounds, unofficial ports, compatibility layers, or alternative versions for unsupported platforms.
