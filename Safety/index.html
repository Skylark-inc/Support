Skylark Safety

Skylark is designed to be used responsibly. The use of illegal content is strictly prohibited across all Skylark services.

Skylark does not support, allow, or tolerate illegal content of any kind.

Skylark Shared Albums

Skylark does not actively scan your photos or personal content. However, this does not mean such content is allowed within Skylark services.

Any illegal content found within Skylark Shared Albums or related services will not be supported, will be removed, and will be reported.

Learn more about Shared Albums content rules here:
Skylark Shared Albums Content Policy

Reporting content

If you encounter illegal content, you can report it directly to @SkylarkSupport.

Consequences

Users who share illegal content through Skylark services will be permanently banned.

In addition, users may be reported and held accountable to the fullest extent of the law.
