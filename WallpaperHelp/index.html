Wallpaper was unable to set

This message appears when Skylark cannot complete the wallpaper process. In most cases, the issue is caused by the image file itself or by the image not being available when the shortcut or service tries to use it.

This does not always mean Skylark is broken. Sometimes the wallpaper file failed to download, became unreadable, or uses an image format your device cannot set as wallpaper.

Possible reasons

  1. The image was not downloaded.
    If the image did not fully download, Skylark may not have a real file to use. Check your internet connection and try downloading the wallpaper again.
  2. The image is corrupt.
    A corrupt image may appear as a file, but your device may not be able to open or process it correctly. Try opening the image in Photos or Files first. If it does not preview properly, download a fresh copy.
  3. The image is not in a supported format.
    Some image formats may not work properly with wallpapers, shortcuts, or certain versions of iOS. For best results, use JPEG or PNG.

What you can try

  • Download the wallpaper again.
  • Make sure the image opens normally in Photos or Files.
  • Restart the shortcut or try again after a few seconds.
  • Convert the image to JPEG or PNG, then try setting it again.
