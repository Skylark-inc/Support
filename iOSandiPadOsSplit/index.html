Skylark for iPhone and iPad.

One Skylark experience. Separate downloads designed for your device, beginning with Edition 26.

Why are there two versions?

Beginning with Edition 26, Skylark offers separate builds for iOS and iPadOS. Although the experience is similar, certain file locations and operations behave differently on iPhone and iPad.

Providing a version for each device helps Skylark work with the correct device-specific environment. You can keep Skylark installed on both devices without repeatedly removing and reinstalling it.

Already have Skylark? If your existing build works as expected, you do not need to remove it simply because separate downloads are available. When installing or replacing a build, choose the version intended for that device.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use Skylark on both my iPhone and iPad?

Yes. You can use Skylark on both devices. Beginning with Edition 26, each device uses its respective build: the iOS version for iPhone and the iPadOS version for iPad.

Keep the appropriate version installed on each device. You do not need to remove and reinstall Skylark each time you switch devices.

Will Skylark ever return to one version for both devices?

Possibly, but we cannot guarantee it. Future changes to iOS and iPadOS may make a unified build practical again.

Any improvement could apply only to newer operating systems. For example, if Apple changes the relevant behavior in iOS and iPadOS 29, Editions 26 through 28 might still require separate downloads. We will evaluate future releases as they become available.

Why does macOS appear compatible? Is a Mac edition coming?

Some iPads identify themselves to websites using a desktop-style browser identifier similar to a Mac. To avoid incorrectly excluding those iPads, Skylark's compatibility detection may also allow macOS devices to proceed.

This does not mean Skylark is fully optimized or officially supported for Mac. Some features may behave differently or may not work. There are currently no plans for a dedicated Skylark edition for macOS.
