Skylark iCloud Album

Skylark iCloud Album is an optional feature that stores images inside your iCloud Drive instead of your device's photo library.

Think of it as a dedicated Skylark image folder stored within your Apple account. This is useful for users who prefer not to keep certain images in their Photos app, or for secondary devices where they want access to images without saving them directly to the photo library.

Skylark iCloud Album uses your existing iCloud Drive storage. No separate Skylark storage account is required.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enable it?

Open Skylark and navigate to:

Settings → Skylark iCloud Configurator → Create Album

Skylark will create the required folder inside your iCloud Drive.

Can my other devices access those photos?

Yes. Any iPhone, iPad, or supported Apple device signed into the same Apple Account can access the folder through iCloud Drive.

How do I remove it if I no longer want to use it?

Simply delete the Skylark iCloud Album folder from the Files app.

Deleting the folder may permanently remove the images stored inside it. Make sure important images are backed up before deleting the folder.

Will I need to buy iCloud storage?

Not necessarily.

Apple provides 5GB of iCloud storage free of charge. However, this space can fill up quickly depending on how many photos you store.

We recommend using Skylark iCloud Album for images that are especially important to you rather than as a replacement for long-term storage.

The folder doesn't work or I can't find it.

Make sure:

  • iCloud Drive is enabled.
  • You are signed into your Apple Account.
  • You have enough available iCloud storage.
  • Your device has internet access.

Important Disclaimers

Skylark iCloud Album requires iCloud Drive to be enabled and available storage space within your Apple Account.
Skylark iCloud Album is not a cloud storage service. It is intended as a convenience feature that uses your existing iCloud Drive storage.

Because this feature relies on Apple's iCloud infrastructure, availability, syncing, storage limits, and account access are controlled by Apple.
