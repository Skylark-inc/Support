Skylark Obsolete

Skylark Obsolete covers compatibility problems caused by older software versions, discontinued Editions, unsupported platforms, and outside services that no longer work correctly on older devices.

Edition 1 has reached End of Life. Devices running iOS or iPadOS 14 can no longer use the original Skylark server workaround.

Current issue

Dropbox and other services have reduced or ended support for some older software versions. This can prevent older devices from opening shared folders, accepting invitations, or connecting to Skylark Servers.

These limitations are controlled by outside services and cannot always be repaired by Skylark.

For iOS or iPadOS 15

iOS and iPadOS 15 may still work, but support is becoming increasingly fragile. Users on these versions should consider themselves on thin ice.

  1. Try opening the Dropbox invitation through Dropbox on the web.
  2. If the browser is unsupported, use a computer.
  3. Use a newer device to accept the folder invitation, then sign into the same Dropbox account on the older device.

If Skylark Servers still cannot be used, Skylark Fe may be used instead. Skylark Fe does not rely on Skylark Servers.

Why this happens

Skylark may continue supporting an Edition, but the apps and services it works with can change their own requirements. When one of those services ends support for an older system, dependent Skylark features may stop working as well.

iOS and iPadOS 16 or later are currently considered safe from this specific obsolete-server issue. This may change if outside services alter their compatibility requirements.
