Why does Skylark mark images as "AI Generated or Assisted"?

Artificial intelligence is making it easier than ever to create realistic images. Sometimes these images are obvious, while other times they can be difficult to distinguish from real photographs. To help provide context, Skylark allows users to mark images that were generated or assisted by AI.

What does the label mean?

An image marked as "AI Generated or Assisted" simply tells you that artificial intelligence was involved in creating or modifying the image.

It does not mean the image is harmful, fake, misleading, or against Skylark's rules. It is simply additional information so you can better understand what you're looking at.

The label exists to improve transparency, not to discourage creativity.

Does Skylark scan my photos?

No.

Skylark does not analyze, inspect, or scan the images you upload to determine whether they were created with AI. Your photos are not automatically checked.

Instead, Skylark relies on the user to identify AI-assisted images.

Because Skylark does not scan images, an image may appear without this label if the uploader chooses not to mark it.

How do I mark an AI image?

1. Rename the image Simply include "AI" somewhere in the image's filename.
2. Upload normally When Skylark detects "AI" in the filename, it can display the image as AI Generated or Assisted.
3. That's it No additional settings or options are required.

What should I do with this information?

Nothing, really.

The label is simply there to help you stay informed. If you come across an incredible landscape, an unusual animal, or something that looks almost impossible, the label lets you know that AI may have played a role in creating it.

Whether you enjoy AI artwork or prefer traditional photography, the choice is yours. Skylark does not block AI-generated images. The label is intended only to provide additional context and encourage users to view online images with healthy curiosity.
