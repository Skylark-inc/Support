Why using your supported Edition is better

While older Skylark Editions may still function, using the Edition designed for your device ensures the best experience. Running an outdated Edition can lead to missing features, reduced stability, and compatibility issues over time.

1. New features

New Editions include features that only exist on newer versions of iOS or iPadOS. When Apple releases updates, new actions are often added to the Shortcuts app. Your supported Edition is designed to take advantage of those updates.

Older Editions cannot access these newer actions, meaning certain features may be missing or unavailable.

2. Stability

Each Edition is built specifically for a certain range of iOS or iPadOS versions. While older Editions may still run, they are not optimized for newer systems.

This can result in slower performance, unexpected behavior, or minor issues that would not occur on your supported Edition.

3. Compatibility

This is the most important factor. As newer software versions are released, older systems lose support—not from Skylark, but from the services and tools Skylark relies on.

If a service drops support for older versions, any Skylark feature that depends on it may stop working completely.

You can learn more about outdated support and limitations on Skylark Obsolete .

What you should do

If you are currently using an older Edition, upgrading is strongly recommended. Your supported Edition ensures access to new features, better stability, and full compatibility with modern services.
