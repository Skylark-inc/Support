Skylark Shared Albums Content Policy

Skylark Shared Albums allow users to share content with others, but all content must follow strict safety, consent, and legal guidelines.

All individuals shown in adult content must be clearly 18 years of age or older.

Adult content

Adult content may be allowed only in approved Skylark environments, such as Sauna. Users must be 18 years of age or older to access or view adult spaces.

Skylark does not promote explicit content. Adult content is only allowed in designated areas under strict rules.

Consent rules

Consent must be clear, voluntary, and unambiguous.

Content that portrays inappropriate, unwanted, coerced, or non-consensual behavior as “liked,” “enjoyed,” or accepted is not allowed.

If a situation would be considered inappropriate, illegal, or non-consensual in reality, it is not allowed, regardless of how it is portrayed.

Age and appearance

Any content involving minors, or individuals who appear to be minors, is strictly prohibited.

If there is any uncertainty about age, the content will be treated as underage and is not allowed.

Age gaps alone do not violate this policy. However, content that suggests exploitation, manipulation, coercion, or inappropriate power dynamics is not allowed, even if all individuals are over 18.

Stylized or animated adult content

Stylized, illustrated, or animated adult content is only allowed if all individuals are clearly and unambiguously 18 years of age or older.

Content that appears underage, uses “barely legal” framing, or makes age unclear is not allowed, regardless of labels, captions, or intent.

AI-generated adult content

AI-generated adult content is not supported through dedicated Skylark albums or official Skylark services.

Users are responsible for how they handle such content outside Skylark services.

Secret groups and coded communities

Skylark does not allow secret groups, coded communities, hidden signals, secret words, hidden pictures, or coded promotion used to share, organize, or promote prohibited content.

Any album, post, link, image, phrase, or community using Skylark to hide or promote prohibited content will be removed.

Not allowed

  • Any content involving minors, real or implied
  • Any content where age is unclear
  • Non-consensual content of any kind
  • Content that disguises coercion as consent
  • Gore or extreme violence
  • Illegal material under applicable law
  • Secret groups or coded communities promoting prohibited content
  • Content that attempts to bypass these rules

Legal compliance

Skylark follows California law. Any content that violates applicable law will not be tolerated.

Reporting

If you encounter content that violates this policy, report it immediately to @SkylarkSupport.

Enforcement

If prohibited or illegal content is found, the content may be removed and the user may be permanently banned from Skylark services.

Illegal activity may be reported to the appropriate authorities and handled to the fullest extent of the law.
